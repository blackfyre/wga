BIOGRAPHY

Vermeer worked slowly and in relative obscurity, producing a small, tightly controlled body of work prized for its quiet domestic subjects and its restrained chiaroscuroGLOSSARY · CHIAROSCURO{{ tipChiaroscuro.definition }}. He spent his entire life in Delft, where he also traded in the work of other painters.

Fewer than forty paintings are securely attributed to him. His reputation was rebuilt in the nineteenth century, when critics recognised in his interiors an unusually disciplined approach to composition, light and colour — closer to observation than to the tenebrismGLOSSARY · TENEBRISM{{ tipTenebrism.definition }} of his southern contemporaries.

COMMENTARY
UNDERLINED TERMS ARE DEFINED IN THE GLOSSARY →

Rather than a portrait of a named sitter, this is a tronieGLOSSARY · TRONIE{{ tipTronie.definition }} — a study of a head in costume. The figure turns out of the dark ground towards the viewer, lit from the left, with no setting or attributes to place her.

The pearl is rendered with two strokes of white impastoGLOSSARY · IMPASTO{{ tipImpasto.definition }} over grey, and dissolves entirely at close range: an economy of means typical of Vermeer's late practice.

Privacy Policy

The Web Gallery of Art is run as a non-commercial educational archive. We collect the minimum needed to keep it working, and nothing for advertising.

01 · DATA WE COLLECT

Aggregate usage statistics — pages viewed, search terms, approximate country — used to decide which parts of the archive to expand. No profiles are built and no data is sold.

02 · COOKIES

A small number of essential cookies remember your session, preferred image size and whether period music is enabled. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set.

03 · THE POSTCARD SERVICE

Sender and recipient addresses are used once, to deliver the postcard, and are deleted with the message when the link expires after 30 days. Addresses are never added to a mailing list.

04 · IMAGES AND COPYRIGHT

The archive is copyrighted as a database. Images and documents may be used for educational and personal purposes only; redistribution requires the permission of the rights holder of the reproduction.

05 · YOUR RIGHTS

You may request a copy of, or the deletion of, any personal data we hold. Requests are answered within 30 days.

06 · CONTACT

Write to privacy@wga.hu. For questions about a specific reproduction, use the enquiry form on the artwork page.
